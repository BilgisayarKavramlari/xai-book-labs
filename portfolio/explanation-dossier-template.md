# Explanation Dossier

Your portfolio for *Explainable AI in Practice*. Pick one AI system in Chapter 1 and add one artifact per chapter. In Chapter 27 the dossier becomes a full explanation audit.

Write every artifact so that you could hand it to an employer, a client or an auditor: say who it is for, what decision it supports, what evidence it rests on and what it does not show.
