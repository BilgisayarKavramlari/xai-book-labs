# Data card: German Credit (Statlog)

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Source | Hofmann, H. (1994). Statlog (German Credit Data). UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5NC77 ; also on OpenML as `credit-g` |
| License | CC BY 4.0 (as listed by the UCI repository; recheck before publication) |
| Size | 1,000 applicants, 20 attributes, label good or bad credit risk (700 good, 300 bad) |
| Used in | Lab 1.2 and later credit labs |
| Known limitations | Old data (1970s-1990s Germany); some attributes combine sex and marital status; the label definition is not documented in detail; the original data come with a cost matrix that treats misclassifying a bad risk as five times worse. Use it to learn methods, not to draw conclusions about real lending. |
