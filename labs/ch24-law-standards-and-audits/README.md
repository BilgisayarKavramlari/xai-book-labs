# Chapter 24 labs: Law, Standards and Audits

| Lab | Title | Kind | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [lab1](lab1/) | Map the rules: an obligation matrix and evidence pack outline from primary texts | No code (browser and template) | about 75 minutes |
| [lab2](lab2/) | Audit a month of adverse action notices by re-performance | Run-only notebook | about 45 minutes |
| [lab3](lab3/) | A Colorado-style disparity test and its explanation | Notebook, two settings edited | about 45 minutes |

All notebooks were run end to end on 2026-11-24 with Python 3.10 on a laptop CPU, each in a few seconds. They use only numpy 2.2.6, pandas 2.3.3 and matplotlib 3.10.9 from `env/requirements.txt`, and nothing is downloaded: the data are synthetic and generated with fixed seeds, so every number in the chapter's Figures 24.4 and 24.6 (`ch24-reason-audit`, `ch24-disparity`) is reproduced exactly.

Legal content in these labs is dated **as of November 2026** and is not legal advice. Laws in this field change often; Lab 24.1 asks you to check every entry against the primary text on the day you do the lab and to record that date.
