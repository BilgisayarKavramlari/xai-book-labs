## 4. Evidence pack outline

- **Ask:** obligation register (this document), audiences, dated.
- **Scope:** system and decision boundary; risk classification and its reasoning.
- **Choose:** method and tool memo (Chapter 23), versions, settings, background data.
- **Test:** faithfulness, stability and usefulness results (Chapter 20); re-performance results (Lab 24.2).
- **Tell:** notices, scripts and instructions for use as sent; readability checks (Chapter 22).
- **Record:** log schema (inputs, model version, explanation issued, human review, complaints), retention periods, who can access.
